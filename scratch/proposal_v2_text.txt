2. Executive Summary 
A modern, scalable and conversion-focused digital platform for BPEL. 
Dcastalia Limited proposes to design and develop a modern, responsive, SEO-friendly digital platform for 
Basic Power Engineering Ltd. BPEL, focused on presenting its power engineering products, electrical 
solutions, projects, services, and technical capabilities. The new website will enhance user experience, 
simplify product and service discovery, strengthen corporate credibility, improve inquiry generation, and 
provide a scalable CMS for future content management. 
 
 
3. Project Understanding 
Basic Power Engineering Ltd. BPEL is a power engineering and electrical solutions company offering 
products and services including transformers, switchgear, electrical panels, busbar trunking, automation, 
complete substation solutions, servicing, upgrading, and related technical services. The website serves as 
BPELʼs primary digital platform for presenting its products, projects, engineering capabilities, corporate 
information, and client-facing services. 
The primary objectives of the website are: 
● Improve overall user experience 
● Modernize the website design 
● Simplify property exploration 
● Increase inquiry conversion 
● Improve SEO visibility 
● Enhance CMS usability 
● Build a scalable platform 
 
 
4. Scope of Work SOW 
The scope of this project covers the complete design, development, and deployment of the website, 
focusing on functionality, aesthetics, and digital performance.
